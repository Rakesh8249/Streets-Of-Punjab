Printer Settings

Configure separate printer names for Kitchen KOT and Counter Bill.

🍳 Kitchen Printer — KOT

Used when you press KOT / KITCHEN on an order. Prints only the kitchen preparation ticket without prices.

🧾 Counter Printer — BILL

Used when you press BILL / COUNTER on an order. Prints the customer's itemized bill and total amount.

Important: The browser does not allow a normal website to silently choose or route to a physical printer. When you print, the system print dialog will open; select the configured Kitchen printer for KOT or Counter printer for Bill. Automatic separate-printer routing requires a local print service such as QZ Tray or a dedicated print bridge on a connected computer.
